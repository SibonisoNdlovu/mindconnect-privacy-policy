Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

MindConnect is a community platform operated by CodePulse Consulting (Pty) Ltd ("CodePulse", "we", "us" or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you use the MindConnect mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MindConnect, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account information

  • Email address.
  • Username and display name.
  • Authentication information required to manage your account.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.

User-generated content

  • Community posts and comments.
  • Private messages exchanged with other users.
  • Profile photographs or other media you choose to upload.
  • Reports submitted about content or users.

Usage and activity information

We track certain application activity to understand how MindConnect is being used. This may include:

  • Number of users who register.
  • Number of users who log in.
  • General account activity and application usage.

This information helps us understand platform growth, monitor usage and improve our services.

Technical information

  • Device and application information, where available.
  • Technical logs and diagnostic information.
  • Information necessary to maintain application security and prevent misuse.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide community posts, comments and private messaging.
  • Display user profiles and community interactions.
  • Monitor registrations, logins and general platform usage.
  • Provide job and opportunity search functionality.
  • Enable reporting and blocking features.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent abuse.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems and improve the application.
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations.

3. Community Posts, Comments and Private Messages

MindConnect stores posts, comments and private messages in its database to provide the community and messaging features.

Community posts and comments are available only to users who are authenticated and logged in to MindConnect. They are not publicly accessible on the open internet.

Private messages are intended for the participants in the relevant conversation and are not available to the general community.

Content is stored and processed to provide the service and may be accessed where necessary for security, moderation, support or legal compliance, subject to applicable access controls.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through posts, comments or messages.

4. Jobs and Opportunities

MindConnect provides a section where users can search for and browse job opportunities and other listings.

Job listings may contain information obtained from external websites or third-party sources.

When users choose to apply for an opportunity, they are redirected to an external website. Any information submitted on that external website is subject to the privacy policy and terms of the relevant website.

CodePulse does not control the privacy practices, information collection or security of external websites. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of external websites before submitting personal information.

5. Sharing of Information

CodePulse does not sell users' personal information.

We may use third-party service providers to operate MindConnect. These providers may process information on our behalf for services such as:

  • Database hosting and storage.
  • User authentication.
  • Application infrastructure and hosting.
  • Technical support and security.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users, investigate abuse or protect our legal rights.

6. Data Storage and Security

MindConnect uses third-party infrastructure providers, including Supabase, to support authentication, database storage and application services.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate security needs.

8. Account Deletion

Users can initiate account deletion through the account deletion option in the MindConnect application.

When an account is deleted, associated personal information is deleted or anonymised where reasonably possible, subject to legal retention requirements and legitimate record-keeping obligations.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

MindConnect is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to address it.
